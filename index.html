About ZoLa Backup HY

An independent, public-data zoning and land-use explorer for New York City. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a replacement for official NYC Planning determinations.

This application uses live NYC Planning ArcGIS GIS services for zoning districts, tax lot polygons, commercial overlays, special districts, and MIH areas. Address search is supplied by NYC Planning GeoSearch. Basemap tiles are from OpenStreetMap.

Because the application queries live third-party services, it is not an offline backup and will depend on those services remaining available. Source snapshots and robust validation would be needed for full operational independence.
